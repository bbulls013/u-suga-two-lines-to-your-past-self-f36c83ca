# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A make.fun entry for the "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty. The program
writes a two-line warning to its past self: line one warns about one specific
moment, line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered.

## The entry

> Don't wave off your father's cough on the drive home that Christmas — turn the car around and take him to the hospital.
> It was pneumonia, and he never came home from that ward, so your last words to him were a joke about the traffic.

The moment is concrete — a Christmas drive home, a cough brushed aside — and
the second line traces the consequence straight from it: the illness, the
hospital ward he never left, and the small joke that became the last thing
ever said to him. No general lesson, no wasted words.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` and to `entry.txt` in this
directory. The output is fully deterministic: running it twice produces
byte-identical files. The entry is 234 characters across exactly two lines,
inside the competition gate of 1 to 280 characters.
